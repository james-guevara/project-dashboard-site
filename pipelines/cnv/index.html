CNV calling

Create and validate CNV calls with PennCNV, PyPennCNV, and PyQuantiSNP.

Validating

A ten-participant G2MH autosomal pilot now has standardized PyPennCNV and PyQuantiSNP outputs and a provisional two-caller consensus. The pilot retained 321 calls after caller, overlap, length, intensity, and problematic-region checks. These thresholds are validation settings—not a finalized production policy—and require calibration across the larger in-house cohorts.
